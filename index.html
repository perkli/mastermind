Mastermind

Crack the 4-color code in 10 guesses. Colors can repeat. Guesses fill from the bottom up.

How to play

Click any hole in the highlighted row to select it, then pick a peg from the tray. Click a placed peg to remove it. When all four are filled, press Guess. Each new guess goes in the row above the last.

Keys: 1–6 pick a color, ← → move between holes, Backspace undoes, Enter guesses.

A black key peg means a right color in the right spot. A white key peg means a right color in the wrong spot. Key pegs aren't in any particular order.
