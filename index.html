Mastermind

Crack the 4-color code in 10 guesses. Colors can repeat.

How to play

Click any slot in the highlighted row to select it, then pick a color from the palette. Click a filled slot to clear it. When all four are filled, press Guess.

Keys: 1–6 pick a color, ← → move between slots, Backspace undoes, Enter guesses.

A black dot means a right color in the right spot. A white dot means a right color in the wrong spot. Dots aren't in any particular order.
